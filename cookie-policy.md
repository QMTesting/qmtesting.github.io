<h2>Cookie Policy</h2>

#### Last updated: October 3, 2026

<br /><br />

This Cookie Policy explains how **QMSTesting.com** (“we”, “our”, “the Site”) uses cookies and similar browser-storage technologies to operate the website, remember privacy preferences, determine the appropriate consent experience, and understand general website usage.

We aim to be transparent about the technologies we use, why we use them, and the choices available to visitors.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device. Websites may also use similar technologies such as browser local storage, scripts, tags, and other storage or access mechanisms.

These technologies may be used to:

- Support essential website functions
- Remember privacy or consent preferences
- Measure website usage and performance
- Help protect website security

For simplicity, this policy may use the term **cookies** to include cookies and similar technologies where appropriate.

---

## 2. Necessary Technologies

Necessary technologies support core website functionality and privacy preferences.

They may be used for purposes such as:

- Basic site operation and security
- Loading pages correctly
- Remembering cookie and Analytics preferences
- Supporting the consent-management interface

QMSTesting uses the **Silktide Consent Manager**, which stores consent choices in your browser's local storage so the Site can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional Analytics.

Necessary preference-storage technologies may remain active because they are required to remember your privacy choices and support the Site's consent-management functionality.

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. If you clear the stored Silktide preference, the Site may treat you as a new visitor and ask you to make a new choice where applicable.

---

## 3. Country Detection

QMSTesting uses a **Cloudflare-based country-detection mechanism** to determine the approximate country associated with a visitor's network connection.

The country information is used to decide whether the Silktide Analytics-consent interface should be displayed before Analytics storage is enabled.

The country-detection process may involve technical information such as your IP address being processed by Cloudflare as part of providing its network service.

QMSTesting uses the resulting country code for consent-management purposes and does not use this mechanism to determine your precise physical location.

If a valid country cannot be determined, QMSTesting treats the visitor as being in a consent-required region.

---

## 4. Regional Consent Approach

QMSTesting currently requires an explicit Analytics choice for visitors detected in:

- Canada
- European Union member states
- Other European Economic Area countries
- The United Kingdom
- Switzerland
- Unknown or unrecognized locations

For visitors in these locations, Analytics storage begins in a **denied** state and remains denied unless the visitor accepts Analytics through the Silktide consent interface.

For visitors detected in other countries, QMSTesting's current regional configuration may enable Analytics automatically.

Privacy laws and regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction and may change over time. QMSTesting may update its regional consent configuration when appropriate.

---

## 5. Silktide Consent Manager

QMSTesting uses the **Silktide Consent Manager** to manage Analytics preferences in consent-required regions.

Where the consent interface is displayed, you can:

- Accept Analytics
- Reject non-essential technologies
- Review or change your preferences

Your selection is stored in your browser's local storage.

The saved consent preference applies to **QMSTesting.com in that browser profile**. It is not stored separately for each country.

For example, changing your VPN from one country to another does not automatically erase a previously saved QMSTesting consent choice.

If a saved preference exists, the full consent banner may not appear again on a later visit even if your apparent country changes. You can use the cookie-preferences icon, where available, to review or change your selection.

---

## 6. Google Analytics

QMSTesting uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand general website usage and improve content, navigation, and performance.

Google Analytics may process information such as:

- Pages viewed
- Visit and session information
- Browser and device information
- Referring pages
- Approximate geographic information
- General interactions with Site content

QMSTesting does not use Google Analytics for the purpose of personally identifying individual visitors.

---

## 7. Google Consent Mode

QMSTesting uses **Google Consent Mode** with a configuration in which Analytics storage begins in a denied state.

The Google tag may load while Analytics storage is denied. When `analytics_storage` is denied, Google states that first-party Analytics cookies are not read or written. Limited cookieless signals may still be sent for measurement and modelling purposes.

For visitors in consent-required regions:

- `analytics_storage` remains denied until Analytics is accepted.
- If Analytics is accepted, the consent state is updated to granted and normal Analytics measurement can occur.
- If Analytics is rejected, Analytics storage remains denied.

For visitors in countries where QMSTesting's current configuration does not require prior Analytics consent, Analytics storage may be updated to granted automatically.

---

## 8. Advertising and Personalization

QMSTesting does **not currently use the consent system to enable personalized advertising**.

The current Google consent configuration keeps the following advertising permissions denied:

- `ad_storage`
- `ad_user_data`
- `ad_personalization`

If QMSTesting introduces advertising, remarketing, or additional tracking technologies in the future, this Cookie Policy and the consent configuration may be updated.

## 10. What Happens If You Reject Analytics?

In a consent-required region, rejecting Analytics keeps `analytics_storage` denied.

While Analytics storage is denied, Google Analytics does not read or write first-party Analytics cookies. Because QMSTesting uses Google's consent-mode configuration, limited cookieless measurement signals may still be transmitted to Google.

Rejecting Analytics does not prevent you from using the normal content and functionality of QMSTesting.com.

---

## 11. What Happens If You Accept Analytics?

If you accept Analytics, QMSTesting updates `analytics_storage` to granted.

Google Analytics can then use Analytics storage and process normal measurement information according to Google's policies and QMSTesting's Analytics configuration.

---

## 12. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where the Silktide consent manager is available, you can use its cookie-preferences control to review or change your Analytics choice.

You can also delete cookies and local-storage information through your browser settings.

Deleting the saved Silktide preference may cause QMSTesting to treat you as a new visitor and request a new consent choice where applicable.

---

## 13. Changing Countries or Using a VPN

Silktide consent choices are associated with **QMSTesting.com and your browser profile**, not with each individual country.

If you make a consent choice while connected from one country and later use the same browser while connected through another country or VPN location, the previously saved QMSTesting preference may still apply.

Changing your VPN country does not automatically clear or reset the stored Analytics preference.
