<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

<p><strong>Last updated: October 3, 2026</strong></p>

<br /><br />

<p>
Welcome to <strong>QMSTesting.com</strong>, located at
<a href="https://qmstesting.com">https://qmstesting.com</a> (the "Site").
</p>

<p>
QMSTesting respects the privacy of its visitors. This Privacy Policy explains
what information may be collected or processed when you visit or interact with
the Site, why it may be used, the third-party services involved, and the
privacy choices that may be available to you.
</p>

<p>
QMSTesting is primarily an informational website focused on software testing,
quality assurance, test automation, development tools, cloud services, and
related technology topics. We do not operate user accounts or maintain our own
customer database. However, technical information may be processed by
third-party services used to host, secure, analyze, and operate the Site.
</p>


<h2>1. Information You May Provide Voluntarily</h2>

<h3>Email Communications</h3>

<p>
If you contact QMSTesting by email, we receive your email address, the contents
of your message, and any other information you choose to provide.
</p>

<p>
Please avoid sending unnecessary passwords, API keys, access tokens, financial
information, confidential business information, or other sensitive information
by email.
</p>


<h3>Comments or Other User-Submitted Content</h3>

<p>
If commenting or another user-submission feature is available on the Site,
information you submit may be processed by the service providing that feature.
</p>

<p>
Depending on the service used, this may include your display name, email
address, website address, comment text, IP address, browser information, or
information used for spam prevention and moderation.
</p>

<p>
Information intentionally included in a public comment may be visible to anyone
who visits the Site. Do not include sensitive, confidential, or proprietary
information in public comments.
</p>


<h2>2. Information Processed Automatically</h2>

<p>
When you access QMSTesting, technical information may be processed automatically
by the Site's hosting, consent-management, analytics, security, or infrastructure
providers.
</p>

<p>
Depending on the service, your location, and your privacy choices, this may
include:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>IP address</li>
  <li>Approximate country, region, or city</li>
  <li>Browser and device information</li>
  <li>Operating system</li>
  <li>Referring website or page</li>
  <li>Pages requested or viewed</li>
  <li>Date and time of visits</li>
  <li>General Site interaction and performance information</li>
  <li>Your consent status</li>
</ul>


<h2>3. Website Hosting and Infrastructure</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting uses third-party hosting, DNS, security, and infrastructure services
to make the Site available and protect it from abuse.
</p>

<p>
These providers may process technical information associated with requests to
the Site, including IP addresses and other network information, for purposes
such as delivering, maintaining, securing, and protecting their services.
</p>

<p>
Third-party providers operate according to their own privacy policies and
data-processing practices.
</p>


<h2>4. Country Detection</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting uses a Cloudflare-based country-detection mechanism to determine
the approximate country from which a visitor is connecting.
</p>

<p>
This information is used to determine which cookie and Google Analytics consent
experience should be presented.
</p>

<p>
The country-detection process may involve the processing of technical
information such as your IP address by Cloudflare as part of providing the
network service.
</p>

<p>
QMSTesting uses the resulting country code for consent-management purposes.
The Site does not use this mechanism to determine your precise physical
location.
</p>

<p>
If country detection fails or returns an unknown country, QMSTesting treats the
visitor as being in a consent-required region.
</p>


<h2>5. Regional Consent Approach</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting currently requires an explicit Google Analytics choice for visitors
detected in:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Canada</li>
  <li>European Union member states</li>
  <li>Other European Economic Area countries</li>
  <li>The United Kingdom</li>
  <li>Switzerland</li>
  <li>Locations that cannot be reliably identified</li>
</ul>

<p>
Visitors in these locations are presented with the Silktide consent interface
before Analytics storage is enabled.
</p>

<p>
For visitors detected in other countries, QMSTesting may enable Analytics
automatically under the Site's current regional configuration.
</p>

<p>
Privacy laws vary by jurisdiction and may change over time. QMSTesting may
revise its regional consent configuration when appropriate.
</p>


<h2>6. Silktide Consent Manager</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting uses the <strong>Silktide Consent Manager</strong> to manage
Analytics preferences in consent-required regions.
</p>

<p>
Where the consent interface is shown, visitors may:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Accept Analytics</li>
  <li>Reject non-essential technologies</li>
  <li>Review or modify their preferences</li>
</ul>

<p>
Silktide stores consent choices in the browser's local storage so that the
Site can remember the visitor's previous choice.
</p>

<p>
Consent preferences are stored for QMSTesting and the browser profile. They
are not stored separately for each country. Changing your VPN location does
not by itself erase a previously saved QMSTesting consent choice.
</p>

<p>
If you delete browser local storage or other Site data, QMSTesting may ask you
to make a new privacy choice.
</p>


<h2>7. Google Analytics and Consent Mode</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting uses <strong>Google Analytics 4</strong> to understand general Site
usage and improve content, navigation, usability, and performance.
</p>

<p>
The Site uses Google Consent Mode.
</p>

<p>
When a page initially loads, Analytics storage is configured as denied before
the Site determines whether Analytics consent is required for that visitor.
</p>

<p>
For visitors in consent-required regions, Analytics storage remains denied
unless the visitor chooses to allow Analytics.
</p>

<p>
If Analytics is accepted, QMSTesting updates the consent state to allow
Analytics storage and sends the applicable page-view information to Google
Analytics.
</p>

<p>
If Analytics remains denied, Google Analytics does not read or write
first-party Analytics cookies. Under Google's Advanced Consent Mode, Google
may nevertheless receive limited cookieless signals for measurement and
modelling. These signals may include information such as consent status,
timestamp, user agent, referrer, screen information, and IP address as part
of normal network communication.
</p>

<p>
For visitors in countries where QMSTesting's current configuration does not
require prior Analytics consent, the Site may update Analytics storage to
granted automatically.
</p>

<p>
QMSTesting does not use Google Analytics for the purpose of personally
identifying individual visitors.
</p>


<h2>8. Advertising and Personalization</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting does not currently use its consent system to enable personalized
advertising.
</p>

<p>
The current Google consent configuration keeps the following advertising
permissions denied:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Advertising storage</li>
  <li>Advertising user data</li>
  <li>Advertising personalization</li>
</ul>

<p>
If QMSTesting introduces advertising or remarketing technologies in the future,
this Privacy Policy and the consent configuration may be updated.
</p>


<h2>9. How Information May Be Used</h2>

<p>
Information may be used for purposes including:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Operating and maintaining QMSTesting</li>
  <li>Responding to emails and inquiries</li>
  <li>Displaying and moderating comments, where applicable</li>
  <li>Determining the appropriate consent experience</li>
  <li>Understanding general Site usage</li>
  <li>Improving articles, tutorials, navigation, performance, and usability</li>
  <li>Remembering privacy preferences</li>
  <li>Preventing fraud, abuse, spam, or security incidents</li>
  <li>Complying with legal obligations</li>
  <li>Protecting the rights and security of QMSTesting and its visitors</li>
</ul>


<h2>10. Third-Party Service Providers</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting does not sell personal information.
</p>

<p>
Information may be processed by third-party services that help operate or
support the Site, including:
</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Cloudflare</strong> — country detection and network or security services</li>
  <li><strong>Google</strong> — Google Analytics</li>
  <li><strong>Email service providers</strong> — communications sent to contact@qmstesting.com</li>
  <li><strong>Hosting or source-control providers</strong> — website hosting and deployment</li>
  <li><strong>Comment or spam-prevention providers</strong>, if such features are enabled</li>
</ul>

<p>
These providers operate according to their own privacy policies, terms, and
legal obligations.
</p>

<p>
Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with
law, a court order, a lawful government request, or to protect rights,
security, visitors, or others.
</p>


<h2>11. International Processing</h2>

<p>
Some third-party providers used by QMSTesting operate internationally.
Information may therefore be processed or stored outside the country where
you live.
</p>

<p>
Privacy and data-protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws
in your jurisdiction.
</p>


<h2>12. Data Retention</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting aims to retain information under its control only for as long as
reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to
legitimate operational, security, legal, or record-keeping requirements.
</p>

<ul>

  <li>
    <strong>Email:</strong>
    Correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to
    respond to inquiries and maintain appropriate records.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Comments:</strong>
    Public comments may remain available while the related content remains
    published unless they are removed or deletion is requested and removal
    is reasonably possible.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Consent preferences:</strong>
    Silktide preferences may remain in browser local storage until changed,
    deleted, or cleared.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Google Analytics:</strong>
    Analytics information is retained according to the settings configured
    for the Analytics property and Google's applicable policies.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Infrastructure information:</strong>
    Cloudflare, Google, hosting providers, and other service providers may
    retain information according to their own policies.
  </li>

</ul>


<h2>13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights</h2>

<p>
Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights relating
to your personal information.
</p>

<p>
These may include rights to:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Request access to information held about you</li>
  <li>Request correction of inaccurate information</li>
  <li>Request deletion where applicable</li>
  <li>Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent</li>
  <li>Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable</li>
  <li>Request portability where applicable</li>
  <li>Complain to an appropriate privacy or data-protection authority</li>
</ul>

<p>
These rights vary by jurisdiction and may be subject to legal exceptions.
</p>

<p>
QMSTesting can generally act only on information under its control.
Information held independently by third-party providers may need to be
addressed directly with the applicable provider.
</p>


<h2>14. Canadian Visitors</h2>

<p>
Where Canadian privacy law applies, QMSTesting aims to provide meaningful
information about its privacy practices and obtain consent where appropriate.
</p>

<p>
Canadian visitors are currently placed in the consent-required group for
Google Analytics.
</p>

<p>
You may contact QMSTesting regarding access to or correction of personal
information under its control, subject to applicable legal requirements and
exceptions.
</p>


<h2>15. EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland</h2>

<p>
Visitors detected in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland
are currently required to make an explicit Analytics choice through the
Silktide consent interface.
</p>

<p>
Analytics storage remains denied unless Analytics is accepted.
</p>

<p>
Depending on applicable law, additional rights may include access,
rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of
consent, and the right to complain to a data-protection authority.
</p>


<h2>16. United States Visitors</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting's current regional configuration does not automatically present
the Silktide Analytics-consent banner solely because a visitor is detected
in the United States.
</p>

<p>
Certain U.S. states provide privacy rights when their laws apply to a
particular organization or processing activity.
</p>

<p>
QMSTesting does not sell personal information and does not currently enable
personalized advertising through its consent configuration.
</p>

<p>
If an applicable U.S. privacy law provides you with rights concerning
information under QMSTesting's control, you may contact us using the
information below.
</p>


<h2>17. Children's Privacy</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children.
</p>

<p>
The Site does not provide user account registration and does not knowingly
request personal information from children through user accounts.
</p>

<p>
If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal
information directly to QMSTesting, contact us so that the situation can be
reviewed.
</p>


<h2>18. Data Security</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting uses third-party platforms and services to operate the Site and
takes reasonable steps within its control to reduce privacy and security risks.
</p>

<p>
However, no website, Internet transmission, browser-storage mechanism, email
service, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely
secure.
</p>


<h2>19. External Websites</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting may link to websites that it does not operate or control. Those
websites have their own privacy practices, cookies, and terms.
</p>

<p>
This Privacy Policy applies to QMSTesting and not to independent third-party
websites.
</p>


<h2>20. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>

<p>
QMSTesting may update this Privacy Policy when the Site, technologies, service
providers, privacy practices, or applicable requirements change.
</p>

<p>
The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
</p>


<h2>21. Privacy Requests and Contact</h2>

<p>
For questions about this Privacy Policy or to make a privacy-related request,
contact <strong> QMSTesting.com</strong> at <strong>contact@qmstesting.com</strong>
</p>


<p> We may request enough information to understand or verify a request where appropriate. Please do not send unnecessary identification documents or sensitive information unless specifically requested.</p>
